# Acceptance testing

- The final stage of testing before a system is accepted for operational use.
- Usually performed by the customer or end users.
- Helps reveal errors or omissions in the system requirements.

## Methods

- Alpha testing
  - Takes place at the developer's site.
  - Uses a controlled environment.
  - Developers may observe users and record errors or usability problems.
- Beta testing
  - Takes place at the customer's site.
  - Runs in an environment not controlled by the developer.
  - Customers report problems to the designer or developer at regular intervals.
