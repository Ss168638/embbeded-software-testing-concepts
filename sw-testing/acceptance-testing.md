# Acceptance Testing
- *final stage of testing process before the systems is accepted for operational use.*
- *tested by customer.*
- *reveals errors and omissions in the system requriements.*

# Methods
- Alpha Test
    - *at developer site*
    - *controlled environment*
    - *developer "look over shoulder" and records errors and usage problems*
- Beta Test
    - *at customer site*
    - *ucontrolled by developer*
    - *customer reports problems to the designer/developer at regular interval*
